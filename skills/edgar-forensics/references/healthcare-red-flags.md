# Healthcare forensic red flags by business model

Framework anchors: Schilit, *Financial Shenanigans* (the seven earnings-manipulation
shenanigans and the cash-flow shenanigans); Cleverley, *Essentials of Health Care
Finance* (provider economics and the ratios that describe them).

## Specialty and branded pharma

| Flag | Where it shows | What would clear it |
|---|---|---|
| Gross-to-net reserve release | Rebate accrual falling as % of gross revenue while Medicaid/Part D mix rises | GtN bridge disclosure; consistency with SDUD/Part D volumes |
| Captive specialty-pharmacy revenue | DSO rising; related-party or concentration disclosure | Sell-through disclosure; channel inventory in weeks |
| Milestone/collaboration timing | Lumpy other revenue hitting round-number beats | Product-revenue-only trend |
| Inventory build ahead of LOE | DIO rising into a known patent cliff | Management explanation with unit volumes |

## Medtech

| Flag | Where it shows | What would clear it |
|---|---|---|
| Distributor loading (sell-in vs sell-through) | Receivables growth > revenue growth; quarter-end concentration | Sell-through metrics; distributor inventory weeks |
| Capitalised development | Intangible additions rising while R&D expense flat | Capitalisation policy note; amortisation schedule |
| Warranty/recall under-accrual | Warranty reserve flat against rising MAUDE events | Cross-read `fda-safety-signals` → maude-recall-risk |
| ASP deflation masked by mix | Revenue per procedure flat while mix shifts | Unit and ASP disclosure by category |

## Tools and diagnostics

| Flag | Where it shows | What would clear it |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument placement accounting | Revenue recognised at placement vs over consumable life | Revenue policy note; deferred revenue movement |
| Reagent rental classification | Lease vs product revenue reclassification | Segment note |
| Academic end-market softness hidden in mix | Revenue flat while NIH funding falls | Cross-read `epi-demand` → nih-reporter-endmarket |

## Services, payors and providers

| Flag | Where it shows | What would clear it |
|---|---|---|
| IBNR reserve development | Prior-period releases flattering current MLR | Reserve development table, not the MLR ratio |
| Implicit price concession classification | Revenue vs bad-debt classification shifting post-606 | Payer-mix and collection-rate disclosure |
| Acquisition-driven same-store masking | Organic growth undisclosed while M&A continues | Same-facility/same-store metrics |
| Physician-practice roll-up economics | Goodwill rising, cash conversion falling | Cash flow from operations vs adjusted EBITDA |
| Risk-adjustment revenue (MA) | Risk scores rising faster than acuity evidence | RADV audit disclosure; cross-read `provider-economics` |

## The four questions that do most of the work

1. Does cash follow earnings? CFO ÷ net income persistently below 1 over several years
   is the most durable single warning in the sector.
2. Is the growth organic? Strip acquisitions and see what is left.
3. Are the estimates under management control moving in a convenient direction?
   Rebates, IBNR, bad debt, warranty, and capitalisation are all discretionary.
4. Did the words change? Compare this year's accounting-policy notes to last year's,
   sentence by sentence. Quiet rewordings precede restatements far more often than
   ratio deterioration does.
